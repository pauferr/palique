# Palique

A desktop instant messenger inspired by MSN Messenger and AIM. Sounds, buzzes, full-screen winks and a cast of cartoon characters: the excitement of getting a message, the way it used to feel.

> **Status:** early development. Invite-only while it's being tested with people close by.

## Why Palique

When you're at your computer, your phone doesn't need to be in your hand.

Palique is a messenger for the time you already spend at your desk. Leave the phone face down and chat the way we used to: a contact list showing who's around, a personal message, the sound of someone coming online, a buzz to get a friend's attention. No feeds and no endless notifications. Just you and your friends, talking.

It brings back the social side of desktop messaging that MSN and AIM had. Being "online" meant you were there, at your computer, and happy to talk. Your status said so.

## Using Palique

1. **Get an invite.** For now, you can only join if someone invites you. The invite arrives by email.
2. **Install the app** from the [Releases](https://github.com/pauferr/palique/releases) page (macOS, Linux and Windows). The builds aren't signed yet:
   - On macOS, open it the first time with right click → *Open*.
   - On Windows, choose *More info → Run anyway*.
3. **Create your account.** Press the button in the invite email, or open Palique, choose *¿Tienes una invitación? Crea tu cuenta* and paste the code. Pick the name your contacts will see and a password. You'll sign in with the email the invite was sent to.
4. **Add your friends** by their email. If someone isn't on Palique yet, you can invite them (you have 3 invites). Whoever joins with your invite is already in your contacts.
5. **Chat.** See who's online and click a contact to open a conversation. Send a buzz to shake their window, or a wink to fill their screen. Set your status (online, away, busy or invisible) and a personal message.

Your conversations stay on your computer: the server doesn't keep them. Your contacts see your name, never your email. You can change your email any time from *Opciones*.
